# Privacy and Safety

- Point the camera only at spaces and people you are authorized to record.
- Make recording obvious; prefer a visible indicator and a physical start/stop control.
- Do not continuously upload camera or microphone streams.
- Default to local processing and short retention.
- Require an explicit action before a frame or audio clip leaves the device.
- Never commit API keys, captured media, faces, voice recordings, Wi-Fi credentials, or device secrets.
- Do not connect motors, relays, high-current devices, or unknown-voltage sensors directly to GPIO pins.
